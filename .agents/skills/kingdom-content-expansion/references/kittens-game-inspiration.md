# Kittens Game inspiration boundary

Borrow:

- slow revelation of new resource chains;
- old resources staying relevant;
- research opening new systems and tabs;
- multiplicative interactions between systems;
- progression from settlement to space;
- long-term optional automation.

Do not copy:

- ordinary-resource storage caps;
- cat/religion terminology;
- exact technologies, costs or prestige systems;
- capacity walls as the primary progression gate.

Kingdom's identity is human civilization, territory and alien conflict.
